- 2026-11-18 · The city map has sound: a speaker button in the map toolbar turns on gentle traffic, birds or crickets, and a soft tone when an agent starts, hands off, finishes or needs attention. Zoom in and you hear that place: at the airport, jets start their engines, roll down the runway, take off, touch down and reverse thrust; the station, marina, stadium and Commons each have their own quiet sounds, and city traffic steps back while you are close in. The background stays a soft, low hum rather than a windy rush. It starts muted, remembers your choice and volume, and goes quiet when you leave the map or switch tabs. (#466)
