# Version: AI Solutions

**Targets:** AI Solutions Engineer, Business Solutions Specialist, Enterprise AI Specialist, AI Consultant, Technical Solutions Consultant

## Positioning
An enterprise technology professional who understands how business users work and
builds AI automations that solve their problems.

## Summary angle
Lead with: years in enterprise IT → AI automation building (Claude, Anthropic API,
Python, CrewAI) → translating user needs into working solutions.

## Section order
Summary · Projects · Experience · Technical Skills · Education · Certifications
(Projects move above Experience when the role is AI-first; keep Experience first for business-analyst-leaning roles.)

## Experience emphasis
Use each position's `emphasis_by_version.ai-solutions`. Feature accomplishments tagged:
`customer-communication`, `stakeholder-management`, `automation`, `documentation`.

## Projects to feature
Projects whose `relevant_versions` include `ai-solutions`, AI automation and
multi-agent work first. Each project bullet: problem → what you built → result.

## Skills order
AI & Automation · Programming & APIs · Enterprise Platforms · Collaboration

## Keywords to use when supported
AI workflow, LLM, prompt engineering, API integration, process automation, requirements
gathering, stakeholder communication, solution design, human-in-the-loop

## Watch-outs
Do not call independent projects "client engagements". Be honest about business-analysis
and process-mapping depth (flag as developing where true).
