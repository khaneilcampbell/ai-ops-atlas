Benjaire Flow · setup checklistAbout 80 minutes

Six steps, in folder order.

01
Add two credentials in n8nBearer Auth named Resend API with your Resend key, and Anthropic named Anthropic with your API key.
5 min
02
Create the tables · 1-tablesCreate flow_settings, flow_inquiries and flow_events from the CSVs, then delete each sample row. Keep exactly one settings row.
10 min
03
Fill your settings · 2-presetsStart from the closest preset, replace every example value, and make an intake key with openssl rand -hex 24.
15 min
04
Import the workflows · 3-workflowsImport 0 to 5. Pick your credentials, set Error workflow to Flow · 0 Error alerts, and set your time zone. Publish 0, then 1 to 4.
15 min
05
Connect your form · 4-websiteUse the Cloudflare Worker (guides/cloudflare.md) or a server forwarder. Never put the intake key in browser code.
15 min
06
Test before going live · 5-testRun curl-test.sh: an owner alert should arrive within a minute. Then work through the 17 checks in test-checklist.md.
20 min
Form returns 401?The intake key in your Worker or server does not match intake_key in your settings. Copy it again with no spaces.
No emails at all?Select Resend API in each email node and verify your sending domain in Resend.

Benjaire Flow · day to day

Once it is live, you only press buttons.

I replied to themStops reminders. Nothing is sent to the client.
Became a clientSends your welcome and onboarding email the first time.
Close: no dealNo reply, went elsewhere, or a test. Nothing is sent.
Every inquiryAn alert with a summary, urgency, budget and a suggested reply for you to review. Flow never sends an AI written reply to a prospect. Weekdays9am, 1pm and 5pm: one reminder for anything past its reply target. Mondays 8amYour digest: new inquiries, wins, losses, time to handle and who is still waiting. If anything failsAn error alert names the workflow and step, with no customer data.
